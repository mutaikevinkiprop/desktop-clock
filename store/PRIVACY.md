# Privacy Policy — Desktop Clock

_Last updated: 2026_

Desktop Clock ("the App") is a desktop clock widget for Windows.

## Summary
The App does **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.
It does not contain advertising, analytics, telemetry, or third-party tracking.

## Information the App Stores
The App stores your preferences (for example: theme, font, time-zone list,
window position and size, always-on-top, and launch-at-startup) **locally on
your own device** in a JSON settings file inside your Windows user profile
(%APPDATA%). These settings never leave your device.

## Information the App Transmits
The App makes **no outbound network connections**. It does not send data to the
developer or to any third party. It does not use AI services, cloud services,
analytics providers, or advertising networks.

## Third-Party Components
The App uses the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, which is a Microsoft
component governed by Microsoft's own terms and privacy statement. The App
itself does not use WebView2 to contact any server.

## Children's Privacy
Because the App collects no personal information, it collects no personal
information from children.

## Your Controls
You can review or delete all locally stored settings at any time by removing
the App's settings file or uninstalling the App. Uninstalling removes the
App and its stored preferences.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL.
